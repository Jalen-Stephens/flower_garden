# Group 5 Strategy Summary (since PrincipledStrategy)

Date: November 7, 2025

## 1. PrincipledStrategy (Initial Creation)
- **Design:**
  - Triad seeding: Places compact clusters of three plants using jittered hex anchors and sampled rotations.
  - Deficit-driven lattice fill: Fills the garden with additional plants based on nutrient deficits, using a hex-like grid and scoring candidates for efficiency and interaction potential.
  - Greedy fallback: Places remaining plants using a grid and a balance score blending efficiency, interaction, and deficit weights.
  - SpatialBins: Accelerates neighbor lookups for interaction scoring.
- **Parameters:**
  - CLUSTER_LIMIT, MAX_TRIADS, LATTICE_JITTER, GREEDY_GRID_STEP, INTERACTION_RADIUS_FACTOR, TRIAD_ROTATIONS (all sweepable).
- **Outcome:**
  - Substantially improved growth and placement over previous Group 5 strategies; competitive with Group 2 on some configs.

## 2. Benchmark Harness & Automation
- **Script:** `benchmarks/run_sweep.py`
  - Sweeps all config JSONs and multiple PrincipledStrategy parameter variants.
  - Supports G5-only and full comparison modes (G2/G3 baselines).
  - Results logged to CSV for analysis.
- **Analyzer:** `benchmarks/analyze_results.py`
  - Aggregates results by variant, computes averages/medians, and ranks by delta vs G2/G3.

## 3. Iterative Variant Tuning
- **Variants Explored:**
  - Adjusted MAX_TRIADS, LATTICE_JITTER, GREEDY_GRID_STEP, INTERACTION_RADIUS_FACTOR, CLUSTER_LIMIT.
  - Per-config sweeps to identify which parameters impact growth and placement.
- **Findings:**
  - Small configs (sapling/shrub/tree) show little sensitivity to parameter changes.
  - Larger configs (four.json, easy.json, better.json) reveal more meaningful differences between variants.

## 4. Targeted Testing Workflow
- **Process:**
  - Run G5-only sweeps first to optimize Group 5 performance.
  - After tuning, run full comparison sweeps to benchmark against Group 2 and Group 3.
  - Use analyzer to summarize and rank best-performing variants.

## 5. Stability & Quality Gates
- **Gardener2 Fixes:**
  - Infinite loop risk in grid generation resolved (while True replaced with bounded checks).
  - All tests pass (pytest: 90/90).
- **Benchmark Harness:**
  - Per-config and interleaved sweep modes; dynamic output file naming.

## 6. Next Steps
- Focus sweeps on larger, more complex configs to surface variant differences.
- Use analyzer to guide further parameter tuning and strategy refinement.
- Document and share top-performing parameter sets for future improvements.
